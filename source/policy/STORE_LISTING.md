💡 ADBLOCKER FAQ
═══════════════════════════════════════

▸ Is this adblocker really free?
Yes. Always. No premium tier. No subscriptions. No ads inside the ad blocker.

▸ Does AIO collect my data?
No. Zero. This adblock extension runs entirely on your device. There's no account, no telemetry, no analytics. We literally do not have any of your data.

▸ Why does an ad blocker need "read and change all data on websites" permission?
To block ads, an adblocker needs to read each page's content (to find ads) and modify it (to hide them). Every ad block extension requires this permission. We use it only for blocking and hiding ads.

▸ Does this ad blocker work on YouTube?
Yes. YouTube is our primary battleground and we update filters constantly to keep the YouTube adblock working, even when YouTube changes their ad delivery system.

▸ Does AIO work on mobile?
AIO is a Chrome desktop ad blocker. Chrome on Android doesn't currently support extensions, but Chrome on iPad and any Chromium-based desktop browser (Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera) should work as an adblocker.

▸ Can I whitelist a site I want to support?
Yes. Click the AIO ad blocker icon, then "pause on this site." Your favorite creators still get their ad revenue.

▸ Will this ad blocker slow down my browser?
The opposite. By blocking ads and trackers, AIO actually makes pages load faster, use less memory, and consume less bandwidth. Most users report 2–3× faster page loads with the adblock enabled.

▸ How is AIO different from uBlock Origin, AdGuard, or Adblock Plus?
AIO combines the filter lists of all of them. Instead of choosing one ad blocker, you get the combined ad blocking power of every major adblock project in a single MV3 extension.
