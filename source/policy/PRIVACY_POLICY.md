## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Automatically Collected (Anonymous, Non-Personal)

When you install and use the Extension, the following Non-Personal Information may be automatically collected:

- **Installation ID** — a random, anonymous unique identifier generated locally at install time. The Installation ID is not derived from, nor associated with, any Personal Information, hardware identifier, account, IP address, or email address.
- **Extension version** — the version number of the Extension you have installed.
- **Browser type and version** — for compatibility checks and aggregate analytics.
- **Operating system type** — for compatibility checks and aggregate analytics.
- **Preferred language** — the locale string reported by your browser.
- **Aggregate blocking statistics** — the number of advertisements, trackers, or network requests blocked. These counts are aggregated and are not associated with the specific sites, URLs, or content involved.

### 3.2 Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you contact us (for example, through our support email, contact form, or feedback channels), we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

- Your name (if provided)
- Your email address (if provided)
- The content of your message, including any attachments, screenshots, or technical details you choose to share
- Any other information you choose to disclose

We use this information solely for the purpose of responding to and managing your inquiry.

### 3.3 Server-Side Logs (Website Only)

When you visit our website (https://aioadblocker.com), our web servers may automatically log standard request metadata, including IP address, user-agent string, timestamp, and the resource requested. These logs are used for security, fraud prevention, and basic traffic analytics. We anonymize IP addresses within ninety (90) days by replacing them with a hashed or truncated value, after which the original IP address is discarded.

### 3.4 What We Do NOT Collect

For the avoidance of doubt, the Extension does **not** collect, transmit, or store:

- The specific URLs of pages you visit;
- The content of pages you load;
- The text of forms you fill out;
- The advertisements you view or interact with;
- Your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, payment information, government-issued identifier, or any other directly identifying Personal Information (except as voluntarily provided under Section 3.2); or
- Any biometric, health, financial, or special-category Personal Information.
